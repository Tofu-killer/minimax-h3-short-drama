# 对白写法速查

```
# 普通台词（动作先于台词，情绪在 says 前）
She sets the cup down, then <Subject 1>, a woman in her late twenties with a low, slightly husky voice (S1), says flatly: <d>[Chinese] 你迟到了四十分钟。</d>

# 听者
<Subject 2> listens with his mouth closed and lowers his eyes.

# 多人齐声
The two children (S1,S2) shout together, <d>[Chinese] 等等我们！</d>

# 画外音 / 旁白（必须声明嘴唇闭合）
The man (S1) says in an off-screen voiceover: <d>[Chinese] 我至今记得那条路。</d> while his lips remain completely closed.

# 跨镜头台词
… (S1) says: <d>[Chinese] 如果我告诉你<scenetrans></d> [Shot 2] At 00:04.500, the shot cuts to a close-up of <Subject 2>; the line continues seamlessly across the cut: <d>[Chinese] <scenetrans>那天我其实在场。</d>

# 片尾被打断
<Subject 1> (S1) says: <d>[Chinese] 你根本不知道他<cutoff></d>

# 音色参考（Ref2VA）
<Audio 1> is the voice-timbre reference for <Subject 1> (S1).
… using the clear, youthful voice timbre referenced from <Audio 1>, exclaims with light annoyance, <d>[Chinese] 嘿！看好你的狗！</d>

# 社区自然语言写法（海螺 App 直接输入也可）
Maya in the green jacket asks in a light, curious voice, "你找到地方了吗？" Leo listens with his mouth closed. After Maya finishes, Leo replies in a lower, relaxed voice, "找到了，就在拐角。" Soft café ambience, no music.
```

规则：一镜一人开口；每 3–4 秒一句或写明沉默；台词保留目标语言；描述段用英文；长句拆段而不是要求说快。
